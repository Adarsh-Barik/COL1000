# Logistics


### Evaluation

The course evaluation policy is:

- Minor:  20%
- Major:  30%
- Lab exams:  13% (2 exams)
- Lab work completion/viva: 10%
- In-class paper quizzes: 15% (3 quizzes)
- Python assessment test/4th week quiz:  5%   (Maximum of the scores obtained in this quiz and the test conducted in July 2026 will be considered.)
- Attendance:  4% (lectures) + 3% (labs)

### Course Policies and Acadmic Honesty

- **Attendance policy**: 4% for class attendance and 3% for lab attendance
- **Policy on plagiarism and other infractions (including LLM usage)**: 
	- Copying weekly programming assignments: 0 for the first case and -1% out of 100% for every additional case.
	- Cheating on proctored tests (quiz, lab quiz, minor, major): F grade and DISCO (which may impose additional penalty). Cheating cases may not be allowed to take the course in summer.
- **Policy on missed evaluations**: (Only based on IITD Medical certificate)
	- Lab submission/viva: Normalization of scores, unless the number of submission is less than 8 which will be handled as exceptions (on a case-by-case basis).
	- In-class quiz: rescaling based on the major exam
	- Lab quiz: A harder lab quiz close to the major exam
	- Minor: rescaling based on the major exam
	- Major: I-grade as per rules of the institute
- **Audit policy**: NA since this is a core course for all
